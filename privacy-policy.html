Your Privacy Matters to Us

This Privacy Policy explains how Install Turf Near Me may collect, use, and handle information when you visit our website, request an artificial turf estimate, or contact us regarding turf installation services in the Dallas area.

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Information We Collect

When you submit a contact or estimate request, you may provide information such as your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, selected service, and details about your artificial turf project.

How We Use Information

Information you submit may be used to respond to inquiries, discuss project requirements, coordinate estimate requests, provide customer support, and improve the website and the services presented through it.

Contractors & Service Providers

Project information may be shared with relevant contractors or subcontractors within our established network when reasonably necessary to review, coordinate, or respond to your artificial turf service request.

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support functionality, understand website activity, remember certain preferences, and help evaluate how visitors interact with website pages and features.

Data Security

Reasonable measures may be used to help protect information submitted through this website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review those policies before providing personal information.
