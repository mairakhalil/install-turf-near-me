Our Installation Process

How Artificial Turf Installation Works

01

Site Assessment & Planning

The area is evaluated for measurements, use,
drainage, access, and project requirements.

02

Ground & Base Preparation

Existing material is removed as needed before
the base is graded, prepared, and compacted.

03

Turf Fitting & Installation

Artificial turf is positioned, trimmed, joined,
and secured to fit the prepared area.

04

Infill, Brushing & Final Review

Appropriate infill is applied when required,
then the turf is brushed and carefully reviewed.
