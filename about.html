Our Process

How Artificial Turf Installation Works

01

Site Assessment & Planning

The area is evaluated for size, use,
access, drainage, and other site needs.

02

Ground & Base Preparation

Existing material is removed as needed,
then the base is graded and compacted.

03

Turf Fitting & Installation

Artificial turf is positioned, trimmed,
joined, and secured across the prepared area.

04

Infill, Brushing & Review

Infill is applied when required, then
the turf is brushed and carefully reviewed.
