Accessibility Statement

Our Commitment to Accessibility

Install Turf Near Me is committed to providing a website experience that is accessible and usable for people seeking information about artificial turf services in the Dallas area.

Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Accessibility Commitment

We aim to make website information and functionality accessible to a broad range of visitors, including people who use assistive technologies or alternative methods to browse the web.

Accessibility Standards

Our accessibility efforts are informed by generally recognized web accessibility practices, including guidance intended to make digital content more perceivable, operable, and understandable.

Keyboard Navigation

We aim to support navigation of important website links, forms, buttons, and interactive features using a keyboard without requiring visitors to rely exclusively on a pointing device.

Images & Alternative Text

Meaningful website images are intended to include descriptive alternative text where appropriate, while decorative graphics may use empty alternative text to reduce unnecessary repetition.

Readable Website Content

We aim to present website content with clear headings, readable text, understandable navigation, and visual contrast that helps visitors locate and understand important information.

Forms & Interactive Features

Contact and estimate forms are intended to use understandable labels and instructions so visitors can provide contact details and information about their artificial turf project.

Responsive Website Design

The website is designed to adapt across desktop, tablet, and mobile screen sizes so visitors can access important content, navigation, service information, and contact features.

Assistive Technologies

Website accessibility can depend on your browser, device, operating system, and assistive technology. Experiences may therefore vary among different technology combinations.

Third-Party Content

Some pages may include links, maps, or other features provided by third parties. Accessibility of those external services may be controlled independently by their respective providers.

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing consideration. We may review and improve website content, navigation, forms, and functionality as the website changes and accessibility needs are identified.

Accessibility Assistance

If you experience difficulty accessing information or using a website feature, please contact us and describe the page, content, or functionality that is causing difficulty.

Alternative Access

If website content is difficult to access, you may contact us by phone or email to request reasonable assistance obtaining information about our website or available turf services.

Report an Accessibility Issue

If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, please contact us and provide details about the page or feature so the issue can be reviewed.
